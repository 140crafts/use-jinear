When you adopt a project management tool, you're betting your team's workflow on
a company you don't control. If they raise prices, get acquired, or shut down,
your data and habits are hostage to that decision.

Open source changes the contract.

## What open source actually buys you

- **Longevity.** Even if the hosted service disappears, the code — and your
  self-hosted instance — keeps running.
- **Auditability.** Security and privacy claims are verifiable, not marketing.
- **Extensibility.** Need a custom field, integration, or workflow? Fork it.

## Our choice: AGPL-3.0

We picked AGPL-3.0 deliberately. It guarantees that improvements made to hosted
versions of Jinear flow back to the community, keeping the project healthy for
everyone who depends on it.

You can run Jinear for free forever on your own hardware, or use our hosted
version if you'd rather not manage servers. Either way, the code is yours to
read.
