This release adds collaborators to tasks and gives the calendar better filters and
drag to reschedule.

## Collaborators on tasks

A task has one assignee. Now it can also have collaborators: the other people who work
on it.

- **Add them when you create a task.** The new task form has a "Pick collaborators"
  button next to the assignee.
- **Change them later.** The task detail screen has a collaborators button. Changes
  show in the task activity, like other changes.
- **Filter by them.** Task lists have a new collaborator filter, next to the assignee
  filter.

## Calendar filters

The calendar side menu has three new filters:

- **Assignees.** Show only the tasks assigned to the people you pick.
- **Collaborators.** Show only the tasks those people work on.
- **Workflow statuses.** Show only the tasks in the statuses you pick, for example
  hide completed work. You can pick statuses from all your teams in one list.

## Drag to reschedule

In the week and two-day views, you can drag a task or event to a different time. A preview
shows where it will land, and the new time saves when you let go.

## AI assistants

The task and calendar tools for AI assistants can now filter by collaborator too.
