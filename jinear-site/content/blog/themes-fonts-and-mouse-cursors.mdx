Until now Jinear came in two looks: light and dark. You spend a lot of hours in a
project management tool, so it should look the way you want. This release replaces
the light and dark switch with themes, fonts, and mouse cursors.

## 64 themes

If you write code, you probably already have a favorite theme. There's a good chance
it's here:

- **Jinear.** Classic Light and Classic Dark, the two looks Jinear always had. They
  haven't changed.
- **VS Code.** The themes that ship with VS Code: Dark Modern, Light Modern, Dark+,
  Light+, Monokai, Solarized Dark and Light, Quiet Light, Abyss, and a few more.
- **JetBrains.** The themes from IntelliJ IDEA and the other JetBrains IDEs: the new
  Dark and Light, Darcula, IntelliJ Light, and more.
- **Popular.** Themes people install on top of their editor: Dracula, One Dark Pro,
  GitHub, Nord, Tokyo Night, all four Catppuccin flavors, Gruvbox, Ayu, Material,
  Rosé Pine, Everforest, Monokai Pro, SynthWave '84, and more.

A theme changes more than the background. Text, links, buttons, badges, text
selection, and the blinking text cursor all take the theme's own colors.

## Picking one

The moon button at the bottom of the side menu is now a palette button. It opens the
Appearance window. You can also get there from your account: **Appearance**, then
**Change theme**.

- **Search** by name, or show only light or dark themes.
- **Preview before you pick.** Move the mouse over a theme, or use the arrow keys, and
  the whole app changes behind the window.
- **Pick** with a click or Enter. Esc closes the window and puts your theme back.

Jinear doesn't switch themes when your device switches between light and dark. The
first time you open it, it starts in Classic Light or Classic Dark to match your
device. After that, the theme is the one you picked, until you pick another.

## Fonts

The Appearance window has a second tab, **Font & cursor**. Inter is still the default.
You can also pick:

- **System**, the font your device uses everywhere else.
- **IBM Plex Sans**, **Roboto**, **Nunito Sans**, and **Lexend**.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible**, a font made to be easy to read.
- **JetBrains Mono** and **Fira Code**, if you want Jinear to look like your editor.

![A note open in a dark theme with a monospace font and the Pixel mouse cursor](https://storage.googleapis.com/jinear-b0/web-assets/blog-posts/jin-574-themes/Screenshot%202026-09-28%20at%2009.31.34.png)

## Mouse cursors

This is the least serious part of the release, and my favorite. There are six mouse
cursor sets: **Cat paw**, **Dog paw**, **Duck**, **Magic wand**, **Pixel**, and
**Mitten**. Each set has its own look for pointing, clicking, and dragging. Text fields
keep the normal text cursor, so typing feels the same.

Mouse cursors only show on devices with a mouse or a trackpad, so you won't see the
option on your phone.

![The files screen in a warm light theme, with the Mitten mouse cursor](https://storage.googleapis.com/jinear-b0/web-assets/blog-posts/jin-574-themes/Screenshot%202026-09-28%20at%2009.32.55.png)

![The new task form in a dark theme, with the Duck mouse cursor](https://storage.googleapis.com/jinear-b0/web-assets/blog-posts/jin-574-themes/Screenshot%202026-09-28%20at%2009.36.11.png)

If your browser supports it, the same tab also lets you change the shape of the text
cursor: a thin bar, a block, or an underscore, the way a code editor does.

## What's not in it yet

Your choices are saved on the device you make them on. Your phone and your laptop
each keep their own theme, font, and cursor. They don't follow your account yet.

You can't make your own theme or share one yet. Themes are built so that this can come
later.

The high contrast themes from VS Code and JetBrains aren't here. I tried them, and
some buttons were hard to read, which is the opposite of what high contrast is for. I
left them out for now.
