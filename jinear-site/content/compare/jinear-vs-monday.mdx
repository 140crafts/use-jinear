Short answer: monday.com is the better choice for teams that want highly customizable boards,
dashboards and automations across departments. Jinear is the better choice for small teams that
want to self-host or pay a flat price for tasks, a calendar, notes and files. I build Jinear, so
read with that in mind.

## The short version

monday.com is a board builder. Each board is a table of items with columns you choose (status,
people, dates, numbers, formulas), and you add views, automations, integrations and dashboards on
top. It is used well beyond software teams: marketing, sales, operations, HR.

Jinear is a fixed-shape tool for tasks, a calendar, notes and files. You cannot add columns, but
there is also less to configure. It is open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable.

## Where monday.com is better

- **Customization.** Columns, formulas, dashboards and views let you model almost any process.
  Jinear's task model is fixed.
- **Automation and integrations.** monday.com can move items, notify people and sync with other
  tools on rules. Jinear cannot.
- **Planning views.** Gantt, timeline and workload views exist on its paid plans. Jinear has no
  timeline view.
- **Cross-department use.** monday.com is built for marketing, sales and operations teams too.
  Jinear is built for small teams doing project work.
- **Company and support.** A large vendor with sales, onboarding and support teams. Jinear has
  one maintainer, so you talk directly to the person who writes the code, but there is no
  onboarding team.

## Where Jinear is better

- **Self-hosting.** monday.com is cloud only. Jinear runs on your own server.
- **Pricing shape.** monday.com sells seats in bundles with a three-seat minimum, so a team of 6
  may pay for 10. Jinear's Team plan is one price for any team size, and self-hosting is free.
- **A calendar and files that come with it.** Jinear's calendar syncs both ways with Google
  Calendar, and its file storage has folders and access control.
- **Less to set up.** You start with tasks, statuses and a calendar that already work.

## What it costs a team of 10

- **monday.com Standard** (billed yearly): 10 x $12 = $120 a month
- **monday.com Pro** (billed yearly): 10 x $19 = $190 a month
- **Jinear Team** (billed monthly): $24.90 a month
- **Jinear self-hosted**: $0, plus the cost of your server

monday.com shows prices in your local currency, so the numbers on its page may look different
from these US dollar prices.

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **monday.com** if you need custom boards, dashboards and automations, especially across
several departments, and you are fine with a per-seat cloud tool.

Pick **Jinear** if you are a small team that wants tasks, a calendar, notes and files at a flat
price, or you need to run the tool on your own infrastructure.
