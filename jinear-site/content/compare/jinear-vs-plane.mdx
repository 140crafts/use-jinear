Short answer: if you want an open-source, self-hosted issue tracker with cycles, modules and
estimates for a software team, Plane is the more capable choice. If you want a smaller and simpler self-hosted
tool for tasks, a calendar, notes and files, with no paid tier inside the self-hosted install,
Jinear is worth a look. I build Jinear, so keep that in mind.

## The short version

Plane and Jinear share a lot on paper: both are open source under AGPL-3.0, both run with Docker,
and both have a hosted cloud. They aim at different jobs.

Plane is a project and issue tracker for engineering teams: work items, cycles, modules,
estimates, intake and pages. It has a company and a large community behind it.
Self-hosting Plane is open core. The free, open-source Community Edition has only the features
of the cloud Free plan. For anything from the paid plans (time tracking, wiki, SSO and more), you
buy a Pro or Business license and run the Commercial Edition, which is closed source.

Jinear is smaller and broader: tasks, boards, a calendar with Google sync, notes and file storage.
The self-hosted version has every feature, with no paid edition and no license key.

## Where Plane is better

- **Engineering features.** Cycles, modules, estimates, intake and a Gantt layout. Jinear has none
  of these.
- **SSO.** Plane offers SAML and OIDC on its paid plans, including self-hosted. Jinear has no SSO
  yet.
- **Size of the project.** A company, a large contributor community and a fast release pace. Jinear
  has been built since November 2022 by one person. Both are AGPL-3.0, so the code stays
  available either way. One maintainer means fewer hands, and also a short path: a bug report
  goes straight to the person who fixes it.
- **More views.** Spreadsheet and Gantt layouts.
- **Import.** Plane offers importers from other trackers. Jinear has no importer.

## Where Jinear is better

- **No paid edition when self-hosted.** Everything Jinear has is in the free self-hosted install.
  There is no feature you unlock with a license.
- **Calendar and files.** Jinear has a real calendar (two-way Google Calendar sync, drag to
  reschedule, an iCal feed) and file storage with folders and permissions.
- **Flat hosted price.** One price for any team size, where Plane charges per seat.
- **Smaller footprint for small teams.** Fewer concepts to learn if you do not need cycles and
  modules.

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **Plane** if you are a software team that wants an open-source Jira or Linear alternative,
with sprints and estimates, on your own servers.

Pick **Jinear** if your team needs tasks, a calendar, notes and files, and you want every feature in
the free self-hosted install.
