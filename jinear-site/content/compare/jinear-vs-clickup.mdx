Short answer: ClickUp has far more features than Jinear, and if you need them, it is the better
pick. Jinear is for people who want a smaller tool that they can self-host and learn in an
afternoon. I build Jinear, so factor that in.

## The short version

ClickUp's pitch is "one app to replace them all": tasks, docs, whiteboards, chat, goals, time
tracking, dashboards, automations and AI, with more than a dozen views. It is a lot of software,
and teams often say it takes time to configure.

Jinear covers a narrower set: tasks, boards, a calendar, notes and files. It is an open-source
(AGPL-3.0) ClickUp alternative for teams that do not need all of that software. You can run it on
your own server, and the hosted Team plan is a flat price.

## Where ClickUp is better

- **Feature depth.** Gantt, time tracking, workload, goals, custom fields, forms, dashboards,
  whiteboards, chat. Jinear has none of these.
- **Free plan for big groups.** ClickUp's free plan has no member limit. Jinear's hosted free plan
  stops at 3 people (though self-hosted Jinear has no limit at all).
- **Integrations and automation.** ClickUp connects to most common tools and has an automation
  builder. Jinear has Google Calendar and MCP.
- **Built-in AI.** ClickUp Brain is part of every plan. Jinear only works with AI through an
  assistant you already use.
- **Apps and maturity.** Native apps, a large company, and years of development.

## Where Jinear is better

- **Self-hosting.** ClickUp is cloud only. Jinear can run entirely on your own hardware.
- **Storage on the free plan.** ClickUp's free plan has 60 MB of storage. Self-hosted Jinear uses
  whatever storage you give it.
- **Less to learn.** Jinear has fewer screens, fewer settings and fewer ways to do the same thing.
  For a small team this is often a feature, not a gap.
- **Flat price.** Jinear's Team plan costs the same for 4 people or 40.

## What it costs a team of 10

- **ClickUp Core** (billed yearly): 10 x $9 = $90 a month
- **ClickUp Business** (billed yearly): 10 x $17 = $170 a month
- **Jinear Team** (billed monthly): $24.90 a month
- **Jinear self-hosted**: $0, plus the cost of your server

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **ClickUp** if you want one tool for everything, you need time tracking, Gantt charts,
dashboards or automation, and you have the time to set it up.

Pick **Jinear** if you want a smaller tool you can self-host, your team needs tasks, a calendar,
notes and files and not much more, and you would rather not pay per seat.
