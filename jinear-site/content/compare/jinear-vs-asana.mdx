Short answer: Asana is the stronger product for structured project management in a company that
needs timelines, portfolios, reporting and automation. Jinear is the better fit if you are a small
team that wants to self-host, or that wants tasks, a calendar, notes and files without paying per
seat. I build Jinear, so read this with that in mind.

## The short version

Asana is built for organizations that plan and report on work: Gantt-style timelines, goals,
portfolios, workload, forms, rules and a large integration list. It is a mature product with a
large team behind it.

Jinear is a much smaller tool. It covers the day-to-day part (tasks, boards, a calendar, notes,
files) and leaves out the planning and reporting layer. It is open source (AGPL-3.0), you can run
it on your own server, and the hosted Team plan is one flat price.

## Where Asana is better

- **Planning and reporting.** Timelines, Gantt, goals, portfolios, workload and dashboards. Jinear
  has none of these. If your manager wants a status report out of the tool, Asana does it and
  Jinear does not.
- **Custom fields, forms and rules.** Asana lets you shape a project around your process and
  automate the boring parts. Jinear has custom workflow statuses and labels, and that is it.
- **Integrations.** Asana connects to most tools a company already uses. Jinear connects to Google
  Calendar and to AI assistants over MCP.
- **Security checkboxes.** Asana has two-factor login, and SAML SSO and admin controls on its
  Enterprise plans. Jinear has no SSO and no two-factor login today. If your procurement process asks for SAML, Jinear will not pass.
- **Support.** Asana has a support team and help for large rollouts. Jinear has one maintainer,
  so you email the person who writes the code. That is quick for a bug, and limited for training a
  large organization.

## Where Jinear is better

- **Self-hosting.** Asana is cloud only. Jinear runs on your own server with every feature
  included, which matters if your data cannot leave your infrastructure.
- **Price at team size.** Asana charges per seat, and many useful features start at Advanced
  ($24.99 per user per month). Jinear's Team plan is $24.90 a month for the whole team.
- **Calendar, notes and files in the same tool.** Jinear has a real calendar with two-way Google
  Calendar sync, notebooks for documentation, and file storage with folders and permissions.
- **Simplicity.** Jinear has less to set up. For a small team that only needs to track who does
  what and when, Asana can be more tool than the job needs.

## What it costs a team of 10

- **Asana Starter** (billed yearly): 10 x $10.99 = $109.90 a month
- **Asana Advanced** (billed yearly): 10 x $24.99 = $249.90 a month
- **Jinear Team** (billed monthly): $24.90 a month
- **Jinear self-hosted**: $0, plus the cost of your server

These two are not the same product, so the price gap reflects a real feature gap too. Compare the
price only after you are sure Jinear covers what you need.

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **Asana** if you run projects with deadlines that someone reports on, you need timelines,
custom fields or automation, or your company requires SSO.

Pick **Jinear** if you are a small team or a solo developer, you want to self-host, and your work
fits in tasks, a calendar, notes and files.

Jinear has no Asana importer. Moving over is manual, or done through an AI assistant over MCP.
