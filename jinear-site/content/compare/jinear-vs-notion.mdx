Short answer: Notion is the better tool for writing, wikis and anything you want to model as a
custom database. Jinear is the better tool if you want task management and a calendar that work
out of the box, or if you need to self-host. I build Jinear, so keep that in mind.

## The short version

Notion is a set of building blocks. Pages, databases and views let you build almost anything,
including a task tracker. That flexibility is its strength, and also why many teams end up with a
task system that only one person understands.

Jinear is opinionated. Tasks have assignees, collaborators, due dates, reminders, checklists,
subtasks and workflow statuses because the product is built around them. It also has notes, but
its notes are simpler than Notion's pages. It is open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable.

## Where Notion is better

- **Writing and knowledge.** Notion's editor, databases, templates and wiki features are far ahead
  of Jinear's notes. If documentation is the main job, Notion wins clearly.
- **Flexibility.** You can model CRMs, content calendars, hiring pipelines and more. Jinear has a
  fixed task model with no custom fields.
- **Views.** Timeline, gallery and chart views on any database. Jinear has a list, a status board
  and a calendar.
- **Built-in AI and ecosystem.** Notion AI, a template gallery and a large community.
- **Apps.** Native desktop and mobile apps with offline support.

## Where Jinear is better

- **Self-hosting.** Notion has no self-hosted option. With Jinear, your tasks, notes and files stay
  on your own server.
- **Task management without setup.** Reminders that repeat, subtasks, collaborators and a
  calendar that shows tasks by assignee or status all work on day one. You do not design a
  database first.
- **Flat price.** Notion charges per member. Jinear's Team plan is one price for the whole team.
- **A real calendar for tasks.** Drag tasks to reschedule in week and two-day views, filter by
  assignee, collaborator or status, and sync both ways with Google Calendar.

## What it costs a team of 10

- **Notion Plus** (billed yearly): 10 x $10 = $100 a month
- **Notion Business** (billed yearly): 10 x $20 = $200 a month
- **Jinear Team** (billed monthly): $24.90 a month
- **Jinear self-hosted**: $0, plus the cost of your server

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **Notion** if your team writes a lot, you want one flexible workspace for docs and data, or
you like to build your own systems.

Pick **Jinear** if you want task management and a calendar that work without setup, you want a
flat price, or your data has to stay on your own server.

Some teams use both: Notion for the wiki, a task tool for the work. That is a reasonable setup.
