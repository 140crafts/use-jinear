Short answer: if you want a mature kanban board with Power-Ups for everything and you are
happy in Atlassian's cloud, Trello is the safer pick. If you want to run the tool on your own
server, or you want tasks, a calendar, notes and files in one place for a flat price, Jinear is
worth a look. I build Jinear, so weigh my opinion with that in mind.

## The short version

Trello does one thing and does it very well: boards with cards you drag between columns. It has
been doing that since 2011, it has native apps on every platform, and its Power-Up ecosystem
covers most integrations you can think of.

Jinear is a smaller product, built by one person since November 2022. A board of tasks is one
part of it, next to a calendar, notes and file storage. It is an open-source (AGPL-3.0) Trello
alternative, and you can run all of it on your own server with Docker Compose.

## Where Trello is better

- **Scale and polish.** Trello has a large team, millions of users and more than a decade of
  polish. Jinear has been in development for almost four years, with one maintainer and a small
  community.
- **Integrations and automation.** Power-Ups and Butler cover Slack, GitHub, Jira, forms and
  custom rules. Jinear has no automation engine and very few integrations.
- **Native apps.** Trello has proper mobile and desktop apps. Jinear is an installable web app.
  It sends push notifications and keeps note drafts offline, but it is not a native app.
- **Free plan size.** Trello's free plan allows up to 10 collaborators per workspace. Jinear's
  hosted free plan stops at 3 people.
- **Extra views.** Timeline, table and map views exist on Trello Premium. Jinear has no timeline
  or Gantt view.

## Where Jinear is better

- **Self-hosting.** Trello has no self-hosted option. Jinear runs on your own server, with every
  feature included and no license key. Your data never leaves your machine.
- **More than a board.** Jinear has a full calendar (month, week and two-day views, drag to
  reschedule), notes in shared notebooks, and file storage with folders and access control. In
  Trello you usually add other tools for those.
- **Flat price.** Jinear's Team plan is $24.90 a month for any number of people. Trello charges
  per user.
- **Task structure.** Jinear has subtasks, assignees plus collaborators, and custom workflow statuses per team.
- **AI assistants.** Jinear has an MCP server, so Claude or ChatGPT can read and create tasks,
  notes and calendar items for you.

## What it costs a team of 10

- **Trello Standard** (billed yearly): 10 x $5 = $50 a month
- **Trello Premium** (billed yearly): 10 x $10 = $100 a month
- **Jinear Team** (billed monthly): $24.90 a month
- **Jinear self-hosted**: $0, plus the cost of your server

For a team of 3, Trello Standard is $15 a month and Jinear's free plan costs nothing. For a team
of 5 on Trello Standard ($25 a month), the prices are about the same, so the decision should come
down to features, not price.

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **Trello** if a kanban board is all you need, you rely on integrations or automations, you
want native mobile apps, or you want a product with a long track record.

Pick **Jinear** if you need to self-host (for privacy, compliance, or because you simply want to
own your data), or if you want tasks, a calendar, notes and files in one tool without paying per
seat.

There is no Trello importer in Jinear today. If you move, you move your cards by hand or through
an AI assistant connected over MCP.
