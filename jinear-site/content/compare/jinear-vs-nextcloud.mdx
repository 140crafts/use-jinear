Short answer: Nextcloud is the right choice if you want a full self-hosted replacement for Google
Workspace or Microsoft 365: files, sync clients, office documents, mail, contacts and video calls.
Jinear is the better choice if task management is the main job and you want tasks, calendar, notes
and files designed to work together. I build Jinear, so factor that in.

## The short version

Nextcloud is a platform. Its core is file sync and share with desktop and mobile clients, and its
app store adds Calendar (CalDAV), Contacts, Talk, Office, Mail, and Deck, a kanban board. It is
open source (AGPL) and has a very large community and a company that sells enterprise support.

Jinear is a single product built around tasks. The calendar shows tasks next to events, notes live
in notebooks next to the work, and files can be attached to tasks. It is also AGPL-3.0 and
self-hostable.

## Where Nextcloud is better

- **Breadth.** Files, calendar, contacts, mail, office documents, chat and video in one install.
  Jinear does not try to replace any of that except calendar and basic file storage.
- **Files.** Desktop sync clients, versions, sharing links and online editing. Jinear's file
  storage is a place to keep and share files, not a Dropbox replacement.
- **Standards.** CalDAV and CardDAV mean your phone's built-in calendar and contacts apps just
  work. Jinear offers an iCal feed and Google Calendar sync, not a CalDAV server.
- **Community and support.** A huge community, years of history, and paid enterprise support if
  your organization needs it.

## Where Jinear is better

- **Task management.** Deck is a simple kanban board. Jinear's tasks have assignees and
  collaborators, subtasks, repeating reminders, checklists and per-team
  workflow statuses, and they show up on the calendar.
- **One product, not a set of apps.** Tasks, calendar, notes and files are designed together, with
  one UI and one permission model. In Nextcloud they are separate apps with separate release
  cycles.
- **Less to maintain.** Nextcloud's app system is powerful, but upgrades across many apps take
  care. Jinear is one stack with one version.

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **Nextcloud** if you want to move your whole office suite off big cloud vendors, or if file
sync is the main need.

Pick **Jinear** if you want a self-hosted tool where tasks are the center and the calendar, notes
and files support them. Running both is also reasonable: Nextcloud for files and mail, Jinear for
planning the work.
